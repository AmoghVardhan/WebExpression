# WebExpression

## Problem
90% of meetings happening in any corporate is done through teleconference tools, be it normal day to day meetings or an executive addressing the gathering. An essential part of any meeting is to understand the response of the audience which can be obtained through facial expressions. With the slow waning of face to face meetings and discussions, it has become extremely difficult for the speaker to understand how the audience is reacting to his thoughts and ideas. Larger meetings hosted by company executives and CEOs have feedback forms sent to the attendees to understand their response. However survey indicates that only 50% of these feedback forms are filled with nearly 10% inaccuracies in the data collected. Hence it has become essential to automatically analyse the expressions and tones of the audience.

## Solution
The solution is to detect emotions from the already integrated face recognition tool in Cisco Webex. When a speaker produces his thoughts and ideas to the audience, powerful deep learning models are utilised to understand the emotions of the audience present. The analysis is done frame by frame for each phrase spoken by the speaker. Six possible emotions are identified namely anger, happiness, sadness, fear, surprise and disinterest from the facial expressions of the audience. A collective score is computed based on the expression of the entire audience and is notified to the speaker at continuous intervals so that the speaker understands the response of the audience and can tune his speech as well as the meeting appropriately. At the end of the meeting a collective feedback is provided to the speaker which could further help him tune his thoughts as well as ideas.

## Disruption Potential
With the advent of teleconferencing and slow waning of face to face discussions, the tool has an enormous potential to disrupt the market in terms of understanding the emotion of audience during meetings and discussions.
